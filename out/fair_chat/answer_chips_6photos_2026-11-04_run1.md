For your concert log, use **#2 and #6** as your design references.

They're the only two that match both of your style chips:

**1. Dark**
**2. One accent**

### 6. CHICAGO on stage - USE THIS
This is your best reference because it's on-topic AND on-style.
* **Dark:** Almost the entire theatre, stage, and crowd is near-black.
* **One accent:** That warm amber/gold `CHICAGO` marquee + the gold frame is the *single* pop of color. Everything else recedes.
* **Why it's perfect for a concert log:** It shows what a crowded venue actually feels like from the audience - dark room, lit stage, silhouettes in the foreground. Great model for hero images, empty states, and cards.

Take from it: near-black background, warm white text, and *one* amber-gold for important things only - like ratings, dates, "now playing", links.

### 2. Silver Porsche from the back in a dark hall - USE THIS
Use this as your *style* backup to #6.
* **Dark:** Black curtain, black carpet, shadows. The car is lit like it's on stage.
* **One accent:** The bright red taillights. That's it. One color telling you where to look.
* **Why it's useful:** It's a perfect example of how to do Dark + One accent in UI: keep everything desaturated grey/silver, then use a single saturated color sparingly for status, badges, or CTAs.

Take from it: how to let photography pop on dark, and how to use red/amber only for the most important 5% of the UI.

### Skip the other 4 for this project:

**1. Grey BMW outside + 4. Green BMW outside:** Both daylight, grey sky, pavement, plus multiple competing colors (yellow car in #1, green + blue + black in #4). That's the opposite of Dark + One accent.

**3. CalmMouse:** Bright white, minimal, light mode. Nice, but directly conflicts with Dark.

**5. Cherry blossoms:** Bright, airy, light-blue sky + soft white. Beautiful, but light and low-contrast - wrong mood for a late-night concert log.

Stick with #6 for vibe/content, #2 for how to execute the dark palette cleanly.
